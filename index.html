Welcome to my website

👋 Hello, I am Toni Rodon, Associate Professor of Political Science at Universitat Pompeu Fabra (Barcelona, Catalonia), ICREA Acadèmia Fellow, and a Delegate for Knowledge Transfer in Social Sciences and Humanities at UPF.

On this website you will find information about my research projects, publications, teaching and other academic activities that I am passionate about.

✨ You can explore the menu or simply scroll down 👇 to learn more. Happy reading!

Feel free to navigate through my Publications. You will find articles on political behaviour, comparative politics or historical political economy, among others.

Discover my Research Projects where I collaborate with amazing colleagues.

Several of these projects have produced new Datasets, which are openly available to the research community.

In the context of the VEARLYDEM PROJECT, we have released a dataset on the election results (municipality-level) during the Spanish Second Republic (1931-1936).

One of my passions is mapping. Check out on the right one of the first maps I made, which shows the spatial distribution of civic associations, factories and religious associations in Barcelona before the Second Republic.

You will also find some cool visualisations summarising our research. For instance, you can enjoy the scrollytelling we created for our female suffrage article.
